How the weighted grade is calculated

Each component contributes its score fraction multiplied by its weight:

Final % = Σ( Weight × Score ÷ Max ) ÷ Σ Weight × 100

Example: Internal 30% scored 24/30, Mid semester 20% scored 15/20, End semester 50% scored 40/50. Weighted marks = 30 × 0.80 + 20 × 0.75 + 50 × 0.80 = 24 + 15 + 40 = 79%, which is an A on the 10-point scale below.
