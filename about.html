About

Built for students, by a student

The CPI Calculator is a simple, fast tool built for GLA University students to calculate their Cumulative Performance Index without the spreadsheet hassle. Whether you're reviewing semester results or planning ahead, this tool gets you an accurate number in seconds.

It has grown into a small collection of student utilities: attendance, SGPA to CGPA, CGPA to percentage, required CGPA and weighted grades. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you enter is stored or uploaded.

Developed by Anurag Tripathi for the student community.
