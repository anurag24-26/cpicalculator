About the project

Free Academic Calculators for University Students

Student Academic Utility Hub is a collection of free online tools designed to help university students calculate academic results, understand grading formulas and plan their studies more easily.

Created by Anurag Tripathi, the project includes a CPI calculator, CGPA to percentage converter, SGPA to CGPA converter, attendance calculator, required CGPA predictor and grade calculator. The goal is to make everyday academic calculations simpler and easier to understand.

Privacy and accessibility

The calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. The values you enter are not intentionally uploaded or stored by the calculator tools. Avoid entering sensitive personal information. Results depend on the accuracy of your inputs and the conversion rules applicable to your institution.

These tools are for educational assistance. Always confirm official academic calculations and conversion rules with your university.
