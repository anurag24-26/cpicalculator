How CGPA is converted to percentage

Universities publish their own conversion rule. Most are a straight multiplier, sometimes with a fixed deduction:

Percentage = (CGPA × multiplier) − deduction

OptionFormulaCGPA 8.0
GLA UniversityCGPA × 9.576.0%
AKTU(CGPA × 10) − 7.572.5%
VTU(CGPA − 0.75) × 1072.5%

The AKTU and VTU rules work out to the same number. Choose Custom if your university uses another multiplier, and add a deduction if it has one.

Always confirm with your university. Conversion rules can differ by batch or year, and the official notification or your transcript decides the figure for applications. The result is capped between 0 and 100.
