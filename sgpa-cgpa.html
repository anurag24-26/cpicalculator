How SGPA converts to CGPA

Each semester's SGPA is weighted by the credits of that semester, so a heavy semester counts for more than a light one:

CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Example: Semester 1 has SGPA 8.0 with 20 credits and Semester 2 has SGPA 9.0 with 25 credits. CGPA = (8.0 × 20 + 9.0 × 25) ÷ (20 + 25) = 385 ÷ 45 = 8.56.

A plain average of the two SGPAs would give 8.50. The two only match when every semester carries the same credits.

SGPA to CGPA: common questions

How do I convert SGPA to CGPA?

Multiply each semester's SGPA by its credits, add the results, and divide by the total credits of all semesters.

Is CGPA just the average of my SGPAs?

Only when every semester has the same credits. Otherwise the credit-weighted formula above gives the correct CGPA.

Can I convert SGPA to CGPA without credits?

If all semesters carry equal credits, a simple average works. If not, use the credits printed on your marksheet.
