How SGPA converts to CGPA

Each semester's SGPA is weighted by the credits of that semester, so a heavy semester counts for more than a light one:

CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Example: Semester 1 has SGPA 8.0 with 20 credits and Semester 2 has SGPA 9.0 with 25 credits. CGPA = (8.0 × 20 + 9.0 × 25) ÷ (20 + 25) = 385 ÷ 45 = 8.56.

A plain average of the two SGPAs would give 8.50. The two only match when every semester carries the same credits.
