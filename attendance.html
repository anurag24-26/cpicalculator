Attendance Calculator

Enter your classes conducted and attended to see your attendance percentage, and how many classes you need or can miss to stay on target.

How attendance percentage is calculated

Attendance percentage is the share of conducted classes that you attended:

Attendance % = (Classes attended ÷ Classes conducted) × 100

For example, attending 28 of 40 classes gives (28 ÷ 40) × 100 = 70%.

Classes needed to reach your target

If you are below target, the calculator assumes you attend every upcoming class. With a target of T%, the number of extra classes you need is:

Classes needed = ⌈ (T × Conducted − 100 × Attended) ÷ (100 − T) ⌉

With 28 attended out of 40 and a 75% target: (75 × 40 − 100 × 28) ÷ 25 = 8. After 8 more classes you will have attended 36 of 48, which is exactly 75%.

Classes you can miss

If you are above target, the calculator finds how many further classes you can skip while staying at or above it:

Classes you can miss = ⌊ (100 × Attended ÷ T) − Conducted ⌋

With 36 attended out of 40: (100 × 36 ÷ 75) − 40 = 8. Missing 8 more classes leaves you at 36 of 48, or 75%.

Good to know

  • The numbers are rounded in your favour: you always get a whole number of classes that is enough to reach the target.
  • Many universities ask for 75%, but some use 70% or 80%. Check your department rules, and ask whether medical or duty leave can be condoned.
