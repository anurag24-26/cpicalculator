How the required SGPA is calculated

Your final CGPA is the credit-weighted average of everything you have done and everything still to come. Solving that for the unknown SGPA gives:

Required SGPA = (Target × (Done + Left) − Current × Done) ÷ Left

Example: current CGPA 7.2 over 80 completed credits, 80 credits remaining, target 8.0. Required SGPA = (8.0 × 160 − 7.2 × 80) ÷ 80 = 704 ÷ 80 = 8.8.

  • If the answer is above 10, the target is out of reach and the tool shows the highest CGPA you can still finish with.
  • The more credits you have already completed, the harder it is to move your CGPA, which is why early semesters matter.
  • The result is an average across the remaining credits, not a per-semester minimum.
